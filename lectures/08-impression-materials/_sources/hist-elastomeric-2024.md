   Polyether polymerization reaction
• It is cross-linking reaction brought about by the action of
  an aromatic alkyl sulphonate catalyst which opens up the
  imine rings and causes addition polymerisation with no
  by-products.
• The reaction is exothermic.
• Addition cured polymerization: no reaction by-
  product
• A too high hydrophilicity is leading to swelling by
  a long contact to water
• The strong acids may cause irritation of the skin
  and soft tissue
• Strong intramolecular forces are leading to a very
  hard polymer: hard to remove from mouth
• Bad taste and odour
• Low setting contraction
• Low tear strength
• Excellent surface detail

• Mixing is easy.
• Low viscosity material in some systems can be
  syringed directly onto the preparations.
• Disposable mixing tips allow for easy infection
  control.
• Cost per impression is higher than other systems.

 Additional Polymerised

• Addition polymerised silicones are superior in
  dimensional stability.
• The permanent deformation and flow of addition
  polymerised silicones are lower in value.

  superior.

  die can only be constructed 2 hours after the
  impression has been removed from the mouth.

• Polysulphides have the highest resistance to tearing

• Condensation polymerised silicones have moderate

                Selection of
            Impression Materials
• Amount of working time needed
• Accuracy and detail reproduction needed
• Type of impression technique
• Number of casts to be made
• Ease of handling
• Compatibility to die material
• Cost of material
                 Disinfection of
              Impression Materials
Dental Impressions need to be disinfected to prevent
cross-infection to the dentist and dental auxiliaries.
     Common Disinfection Agents
• Sodium hypochlorite
• Glutaraldehyde
• Iodoform
                 Disinfection of
              Impression Materials
• Most impression materials can be placed in
  disinfecting solution for 10 minutes without
  significant changes in properties.
• However, if polyethers are soaked for more than 10
  minutes, water imbibition may occur.
