    NON ELASTIC
IMPRESSION MATERIALS
IMPRESSION
  PLASTER
IMPRESSION PLASTER
▪ Plaster of Paris
▪ Modifiers
▪ Potato Starch
USES
▪ Impression of edentulous areas with minimal
  undercuts.
▪ Registration of jaw relations
▪ Securing of clutches for jaw tracings
CHARACTERISTICS
▪ Low strength
▪ Brittle
▪ Short setting time
▪ Dimensionally stable
▪ Minimal distortion of soft tissues
IMPRESSION COMPOUND
They are thermoplastic materials i.e. they soften
on being heated and harden on cooling down to
mouth or room temperature.
TYPES OF COMPOUND
USES
▪ Impression of edentulous areas
▪ As a tray material
▪ Impression of single tooth preparation
▪ Border moulding of impression trays in complete
  denture construction (Tracing stick compound)
REQUIREMENTS
▪ It should harden completely at, or slightly above mouth
  temperature.
▪ It should be plastic at a temperature which will not cause
  discomfort to the patient or injuries to the tissues.
▪ The material should harden uniformly without distortion when
  it is cooled.
▪ When it is soft, the material should have a consistency which
  will allow it to reproduce fine details and to retain them after
  hardening.
REQUIREMENTS
▪ On withdrawal from the mouth, the material should
  not deform or fracture.
▪ Its surface should exhibit a smooth, glossy
  appearance after it has been passed through a
  flame.
▪ After heating, it should withstand trimming with a
  sharp knife without flaking or chipping.
COMPOSITION
▪ Waxes
▪ Synthetic or natural resins
▪ Stearic acid, shellac or gutta percha – plasticizers
▪ French chalk - filler
THERMAL PROPERTIES
▪ Low thermal conductivity
▪ High co-efficient of thermal expansion
FLOW
▪ Adequate flow to allow impression to be made.
▪ Minimal flow on hardening to prevent distortion.
TYPE I – IMPRESSION COMPOUND
Not more than 6% flow at mouth temperature
and not more than 85% flow at 45°C.
TYPE II – TRAY COMPOUND
Not more than 2% flow at 37°C and not less
than 70% nor more than 85% flow at 45°C.
CHARACTERISTICS
DISTORTION
Being a thermoplastic material, compound would
distort on storage.
CAUSES OF DISTORTION
▪ Improper softening of the material
▪ Removal of the impression from the mouth before it is
  thoroughly chilled.
▪ Not making the cast after impression is made
SOFTENING METHODS
▪ Dry heat
▪ Water bath
